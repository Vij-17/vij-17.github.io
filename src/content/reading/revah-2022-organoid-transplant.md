---
title: Maturation and circuit integration of transplanted human cortical organoids
authors: Revah, O. et al.
venue: Nature
year: 2022
url: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05277-w
added: 2026-11-06
---

Human cortical organoids transplanted into the somatosensory cortex of newborn rats matured further than organoids kept in a dish, received thalamic input, responded to whisker stimulation and could drive reward-seeking behaviour. The study is the reason I became interested in plasticity: a host brain incorporated foreign neurons into its circuits and learned to use their activity.
